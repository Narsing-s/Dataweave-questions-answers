# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- explain technical disagreements with evidence;
- help beginners understand DataWeave concepts;
- give actionable review feedback;
- keep discussions focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.
